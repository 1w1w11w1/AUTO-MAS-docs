This section sets the ownership framework and use principles for Section 4.1 visual assets.

### 3.1 Scope

The substantive terms of this Agreement (ownership, licenses, commercial restrictions, redistribution duties, and the like) **bind only the visual assets listed in Section 4.1**. Section 4.2 externally introduced resources are not bound by those substantive terms.

### 3.2 Copyright ownership

Copyright in each visual asset vests in the party named under “Copyright holder” in the Section 4.1 main table. No one may claim any copyright or right to use a visual asset solely because “the code is open source” or “the asset ships with open-source software.”

### 3.3 Moral rights

Moral rights **remain with the author forever** and do not transfer by assignment, license, or distribution. Every use must respect the author’s moral rights.

### 3.4 Allocation of economic rights

The ownership and exercise of economic rights are determined by the Section 4.1.1 schedule corresponding to the main-table “Rights-allocation ID.” Core principles:

- The project team may not grant users any license beyond the rights the project team itself holds.
- Rights dimensions not listed in the schedule remain with the author by default; neither the project team nor end users hold them.

### 3.5 Commercial use

Commercial-use licensing for Section 4.1 visual assets is exercised uniformly by the commercial rights contact. **No party may use the relevant assets for commercial purposes** without that contact’s prior written (including verifiable electronic) authorization.

### 3.6 Non-commercial use

Subject to Section 2 redistribution duties and the main-table attribution requirements, end users may use Section 4.1 assets for personal study, non-profit research, or non-commercial display to the extent allowed by the applicable license type. Precise boundaries follow the corresponding rights-allocation schedule.

### 3.7 No brand confusion

Even with a non-commercial license, brand-mark assets must not be used in a manner likely to cause the public to believe there is sponsorship, endorsement, affiliation, or official partnership with the AUTO-MAS project team.
